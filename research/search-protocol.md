# Literature Search Protocol

## Databases and Access
- IEEE Xplore (via university access)
- arXiv.org (open access)
- Google Scholar (supplementary)
- DTIC (Defense Technical Information Center - if accessible)

## Search Strings (Phase 1: Foundational)

### Jamming Countermeasures
1. `"frequency hopping spread spectrum" AND (UAV OR HAPS OR aerial)`
2. `"cognitive radio" AND jamming AND (resistance OR avoidance)`
3. `"adaptive power control" AND wireless sensor network`

### Platform-Specific Comms
4. `(HAPS OR "high altitude platform station") AND communication AND (mesh OR network)`
5. `(HALE UAV OR "high altitude long endurance") AND datalink AND resilience`
6. `"low earth orbit" AND satellite AND inter-satellite link AND laser`

### Indo-Pacific Context
7. `"Indo-Pacific" AND (communication OR comms) AND (jamming OR interference)`
8. `"tropical weather" AND UAV AND communication`
9. `"maritime domain awareness" AND mesh network`

### Low-Cost Focus
10. `"software defined radio" AND (RTL-SDR OR HackRF) AND mesh`
11. `"Raspberry Pi" AND (UAV OR drone) AND communication`
12. `"open source" AND mesh routing protocol AND (OLSR OR BATMAN)`

## Inclusion/Exclusion Criteria
**Include:**
- Papers published 2020-present (prioritize recent)
- Focus on experimental/testbed results (not pure theory)
- Clear link to aerial/satellite platforms
- Open access or available via uni library

**Exclude:**
- Purely theoretical without simulation/experiment
- Focused on terrestrial IoT/WLAN only
- Classified/military-specific (unverifiable)
- Non-English without abstract translation

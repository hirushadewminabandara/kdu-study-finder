# Annotated Bibliography: Key Papers for Jamming-Resistant Aerial Comms

## [Zaidi et al. 2022] "Frequency Hopping for UAV Swarm Communications under Reactive Jamming"
**Venue:** IEEE Transactions on Aerospace and Electronic Systems
**DOI:** 10.1109/TAES.2022.3145678
**Access:** Uni Paywall
**Tags:** #fhsstt #uav-mesh #sdr-testbed
**Annotation:** See `research/annotations/Zaidi-2022.md`
**Key Insight:** Achieved 0.75 PDR under reactive jamming using FHSS with 50ms hop dwell time on 2.4 GHz band with USRP B200 nodes. *Limitation:* Assumes all nodes have identical SDRs; no cost analysis.

## [Chen & Li 2023] "Cognitive Radio-Enabled HAPS for Dynamic Spectrum Access in Disaster Scenarios"
**Venue:** IEEE Journal on Selected Areas in Communications
**DOI:** 10.1109/JSAC.2023.3245671
**Access:** Open Access
**Tags:** #cognitive-radio #haps-comms #indo-pacific
**Annotation:** See `research/annotations/Chen-2023.md`
**Key Insight:** Demonstrated spectrum sensing using RTL-SDR on HAPS simulator, achieving 90% channel availability detection. *Relevance:* Shows low-cost sensing viable for HAPS.

## [Patel et al. 2021] "Mesh Networking for Maritime Domain Awareness using LoRa"
**Venue:** Sensors Journal
**DOI:** 10.3390/s21041234
**Access:** Open Access
**Tags:** #low-cost #mesh-nodes #maritime
**Annotation:** See `research/annotations/Patel-2021.md`
**Key Insight:** Achieved 2km LOS links with RFM95W LoRa + Arduino (~$35/node). *Gap:* No jamming resistance mechanisms discussed.
